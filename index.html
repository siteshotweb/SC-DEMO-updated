The property

Considered space, quietly stated.

Set back from the street beneath a canopy of mature trees, 119 Willowbrook trades the noise of the city for the quiet architecture of a life well kept. Four bedrooms and three and a half baths unfold across a layout built for both quiet routine and easy entertaining.

The kind of stillness that only comes with room to breathe.

The main floor is anchored by a vaulted great room opening onto a chef's kitchen finished in quartz and white oak. Upstairs, the primary suite offers a private sitting area, walk-in closet, and spa-inspired ensuite, while a finished lower level adds a family room, office, and direct walkout to the landscaped, fenced yard.
